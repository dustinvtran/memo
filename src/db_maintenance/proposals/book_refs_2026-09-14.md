# English editions proposed for the books filed under another book's ISBN

#344. 147 book(s) the metadata backfill refuses because the ISBN they are filed under names a different title — usually the same book in another language. 67 have at least one English edition that clears every filter; 35 have none; 45 could not be searched.

> **45 of these were never actually searched.** Google Books rate-limited the run, and a refused search comes back looking exactly like one that found nothing. They are marked "Not searched" below rather than counted as books with no English edition — do not read the absence of a candidate there as an answer. `--retry` finishes them once the quota resets.

**Nothing here has been written.** To approve one, set `approved` to the ISBN you have checked in `book_refs_2026-09-14.json`, leave the rest `null`, and run `node scripts/propose_book_refs.js --apply --from=<that file>`. A repoint also clears `duration`, `imageUrl`, `metadataUpdatedDate` so the next backfill refills them from the new edition; the stored `releaseYear`, `authors`, `genres` and `publishers` are left alone.
